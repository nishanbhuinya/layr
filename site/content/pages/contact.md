# Contact and grievances

Last updated 30 September 2026.

## Contact

- A bug or a question about LAYR: [open an issue](https://github.com/nishanbhuinya/layr/issues).
- Anything else, including privacy: [official@dynshift.com](mailto:official@dynshift.com).

## Grievance Officer

- **Grievance Officer, DynShift**
- Email: [official@dynshift.com](mailto:official@dynshift.com?subject=Grievance)
- Based in India.

Handles complaints and requests about personal data on all DynShift sites: access, correction, erasure, withdrawal of consent, nomination, and anything else about how your data is handled.

## How a request is handled

1. Write to the address above, saying what you want done. For analytics, include your Google Analytics client identifier if you have it; nothing else identifies you there.
2. You get an acknowledgement, and a full answer within **30 days**.
3. We may ask for just enough to confirm the request is yours, and will never ask for a password or an identity document for this.
4. If you are not satisfied: in India, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India after this grievance process; in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, to your data protection authority.

Requests about Google's advertising data are Google's to answer, through [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/) and Google's own privacy tools.

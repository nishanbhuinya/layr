# Privacy

layr.dynshift.com is a static site. There are no accounts, no forms that send data, and no server of ours that sees your requests beyond GitHub Pages, which hosts the files.

## Analytics

When analytics is enabled, the site asks before loading Google Analytics. Nothing is loaded until you accept, and declining is remembered in your browser. Analytics counts page views so the documentation can be improved.

## Advertising

Some pages may show Google AdSense ads, never inside documentation content, code or the playground. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google shows its own consent message before personalised advertising. You can change your choices at any time with **Privacy settings** in the footer.

## What stays in your browser

Your theme choice, your analytics answer, and the code in a playground link (it lives in the URL itself) stay on your device. Nothing you type in the playground is sent anywhere: the compiler runs in your browser.

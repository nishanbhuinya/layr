# Cookies

Last updated 30 September 2026.

This site sets no cookies of its own. It keeps a few entries in your browser's local storage, listed first. Every cookie is set by Google, and only when the conditions in the last column are met. Change your answer at any time with **Privacy settings** in the footer.

| Name | Set by | Purpose | Lasts | When |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `dynshift-consent` (local storage) | This site | Remembers your analytics choice. Never sent to a server. | Until cleared; asked again after 12 months | When you answer |
| `layr-theme`, `layr-palette` (local storage) | This site | Remember your light or dark theme and palette | Until you change or clear them | When you pick a theme |
| `FCCDCF`, `FCNEC` | Google (consent message) | Store your answer to Google's consent message | Up to 13 months | EEA, UK and Switzerland, when you answer |
| `__gads`, `__gpi` | Google AdSense | Serve ads, limit repeats, measure them | 13 months | EEA, UK, Switzerland: only with consent. Elsewhere: when an ad is shown |
| `__eoi` | Google AdSense | Security and fraud prevention for ads | 6 months | As above |
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Google Analytics | Tell visits apart and keep a session together, to count visits | 2 years | Only when analytics is on for this site and you allow it; never under Global Privacy Control |
| Google's own cookies, such as `IDE` and `NID` | Google, on its own domains | Advertising, including personalisation where allowed | Up to 13 months | Governed by Google; see [Google's cookie list](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies) |

## Refusing them

Say no in Google's consent message or in this site's own question, turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser, block third-party cookies, or use an ad blocker. The site works the same in every case. More in the [privacy policy](/privacy#your-choices).
